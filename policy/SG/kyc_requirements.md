# Singapore KYC Requirements

## Source: MAS Notice 626 (Prevention of Money Laundering and Countering the Financing of Terrorism)

### Certificate of Incorporation (ACRA)
All corporate customers must provide proof of legal existence. The Certificate of Incorporation from ACRA (Accounting and Corporate Regulatory Authority) or recent BizFile extract establishes the legal entity and UEN.

**Required fields:**
- Company name (legal name as registered)
- UEN (Unique Entity Number)
- Date of incorporation
- Registered address

### Board Resolution
A board resolution authorizing account opening and specifying authorized signatories is required. This ensures the bank has proper mandate documentation.

**Required fields:**
- Company name
- Resolution date (not older than 6 months)
- Authorized signatories named

### UBO Declaration
For AML/CFT compliance, banks must identify beneficial owners holding 25% or more ownership (or controlling interest). A UBO declaration captures beneficial ownership structure.

**Required fields:**
- UBO name(s)
- Date of birth
- Ownership percentage or control mechanism

### Proof of Address
Verification of the registered business address is required. Utility bills or bank statements not older than 3 months are acceptable.

**Required fields:**
- Address matching ACRA records
- Document date within last 3 months

### Risk-Based Approach
Singapore follows a risk-based approach:
- Low risk: Standard CDD (Customer Due Diligence)
- Medium/High risk: Enhanced CDD including source of funds verification
- PEP screening required for all UBOs and directors
