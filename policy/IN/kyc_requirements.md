# India KYC Requirements

## Source: RBI KYC Master Direction 2016 (amended)

### Certificate of Incorporation
All corporate customers must provide proof of legal existence. The Certificate of Incorporation issued by the Registrar of Companies (RoC) establishes the legal entity and registration details (CIN). This is mandatory for all company onboarding.

**Required fields:**
- Company name (legal name as registered)
- CIN (Corporate Identification Number)
- Date of incorporation
- Registered address

### Directors List
Banks must verify the identity of all directors and authorized signatories. A directors list or company profile establishes who has authority to operate accounts and sign documents.

**Required fields:**
- Names of all current directors
- Company name for cross-reference

### UBO Declaration
For anti-money laundering compliance, banks must identify and verify Ultimate Beneficial Owners (UBOs) holding 10% or more ownership. A UBO declaration captures beneficial ownership structure.

**Required fields:**
- UBO name(s)
- Date of birth (for identity verification)
- Nationality (if available)

### Proof of Address
Verification of the registered business address is required. Utility bills (electricity, water, telephone) not older than 3 months are acceptable.

**Required fields:**
- Address matching the registered office
- Bill date within last 3 months

### Risk-Based Approach
- Low risk: Standard documentation
- Medium risk: Enhanced verification of UBOs
- High risk: Additional documentation and senior approval required
